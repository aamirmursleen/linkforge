# Getting Started with LinkForge

## Welcome to LinkForge!

This guide will help you get up and running in just a few minutes.

## Step 1: Access Your Dashboard

After signing in, you'll see your Dashboard. This is your home base showing:
- Quick stats (total clicks, active links, QR codes)
- Recent links
- Quick action buttons

## Step 2: Create Your First Short Link

1. Click **"Create Link"** or go to **Links** in the sidebar
2. Paste your long URL in the "Destination URL" field
3. (Optional) Add a title to remember what this link is for
4. (Optional) Set a custom alias like "my-promo"
5. Click **"Create Link"**
6. Done! Copy your new short link and share it

## Step 3: Create a QR Code

1. Go to **QR Code** in the sidebar
2. Select QR type (URL is most common)
3. Enter your URL or content
4. Customize colors and style
5. Click **"Download PNG"** or **"Download SVG"**
6. Print or share your QR code!

## Step 4: View Your Analytics

1. Go to **Analytics** in the sidebar
2. See overall statistics
3. Click on any link to see specific analytics
4. Use time range filters (24H, 7D, 30D, etc.)

## Step 5: Build a Bio Page (Optional)

1. Go to **Bio Pages** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Create New Page"**
3. Add your links and content
4. Choose a theme
5. Publish and share your bio page URL

## Dashboard Navigation

### Sidebar Menu
- **Dashboard** - Overview and quick actions
- **Links** - Manage all your short links
- **QR Code** - Create and manage QR codes
- **Analytics** - View click statistics
- **Bio Pages** - Build link-in-bio pages
- **UTM Builder** - Create tracked URLs
- **Domains** - Custom domain settings
- **Settings** - Account and preferences

## Quick Tips

### Organizing Links
- Use **folders** to group related links
- Add **tags** for easy filtering
- Use descriptive **titles** you'll remember

### Maximizing Analytics
- Add **UTM parameters** to track campaigns
- Use **unique links** for each platform
- Check analytics **weekly** to optimize

### Sharing Links
- Use **short links** on social media
- Put **QR codes** on printed materials
- Add your **bio page** to all profiles
