# Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
Track every click on your links with detailed analytics. Understand your audience, optimize campaigns, and measure performance.

## Dashboard Metrics

### Total Clicks
The total number of times your links were clicked in the selected time period.

### Unique Visitors
Number of different people who clicked (based on IP hashing for privacy).

### Top Countries
Geographic breakdown of where your clicks come from.

### Top Referrers
Which websites and platforms are sending traffic to your links.

## Time Ranges

Select from predefined time ranges:
- **1H** - Last 1 hour
- **24H** - Last 24 hours
- **7D** - Last 7 days
- **30D** - Last 30 days
- **90D** - Last 90 days

## Analytics Breakdown

### Geographic Data
- **Country** - Which countries your visitors are from
- **Region/State** - Sub-country location
- **City** - Specific city location

### Device Analytics
- **Device Type**: Mobile, Desktop, Tablet
- **Operating System**: iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux
- **Browser**: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, Opera

### Traffic Sources
- **Direct** - No referrer, typed URL directly
- **Google** - From Google search or ads
- **Facebook** - From Facebook posts or ads
- **Twitter/X** - From Twitter posts
- **Instagram** - From Instagram bio or stories
- **LinkedIn** - From LinkedIn posts
- **Email** - From email campaigns
- **Other** - Other websites

### Click Timeline
- **Hourly Heatmap** - See which hours get the most clicks
- **Daily Trend** - Click pattern over days
- **Peak Times** - Identify best times to share

## How to View Analytics

### Overall Analytics
1. Go to **Dashboard → Analytics**
2. Select time range (1H, 24H, 7D, 30D, 90D)
3. View charts and metrics

### Link-Specific Analytics
1. Go to **Dashboard → Links**
2. Click on any link
3. View analytics for that specific link

## Metrics Explained

### Click Rate
Percentage of people who clicked vs. viewed the link.

### Bounce Detection
We track if users immediately leave after clicking.

### Bot Filtering
We automatically detect and filter out bot traffic from:
- Googlebot, Bingbot, Yahoo Slurp
- Facebook, Twitter, LinkedIn crawlers
- SEO tools and monitoring services
- 30+ known bot patterns

## UTM Attribution

When you add UTM parameters to your links, analytics show:
- **Source** breakdown (facebook, google, newsletter)
- **Medium** breakdown (cpc, organic, email)
- **Campaign** performance comparison

## Exporting Data

1. Go to Analytics dashboard
2. Click **"Export"** button
3. Choose format:
   - CSV for spreadsheets
   - JSON for developers
4. Download your data

## Privacy Compliance

- We hash IP addresses - never store raw IPs
- GDPR compliant data handling
- No personal data collection
- Aggregated statistics only

## Real-time Updates

Analytics update in near real-time:
- New clicks appear within seconds
- Charts refresh automatically
- No manual refresh needed

## Comparison Mode

Compare current period with previous period:
- See growth percentage
