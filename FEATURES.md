# LinkForge - Features Guide

Welcome to LinkForge! This guide will help you understand all the features and how to use them.

---

## Part 1: All Features

### 1. Link Shortener
Make long links short and easy to share.

**What it does:**
- Turn a long URL into a short one
- Add a custom name to your link (like "my-sale" instead of random letters)
- Set a password so only some people can open it
- Set an end date so the link stops working after some time
- See how many people clicked your link

---

### 2. QR Code Maker
Make QR codes that people can scan with their phone.

**Types of QR codes you can make:**
- **Website Link** - Opens a website when scanned
- **Contact Card** - Saves your phone number and name to their phone
- **WiFi** - Connects to WiFi without typing password
- **SMS** - Opens a text message ready to send
- **Email** - Opens an email ready to send
- **Plain Text** - Shows a message

**You can also:**
- Pick colors for your QR code
- Add a frame around it
- Download it as a picture (PNG or SVG)

---

### 3. Analytics (Stats)
See how your links are doing.

**What you can see:**
- Total clicks on all your links
- Which countries people are from
- What device they use (phone, computer, tablet)
- What browser they use (Chrome, Safari, Firefox)
- Where people came from (Google, Facebook, Twitter)
- A heat map showing when people click the most

**Time options:**
- Last 1 hour
- Last 24 hours
- Last 7 days
- Last 30 days
- Last 90 days

---

### 4. UTM Builder
Add tracking tags to your links to see where clicks come from.

**What is UTM?**
UTM tags help you know which ad or post brought someone to your website.

**Tags you can add:**
- **Source** - Where the link is shared (like Facebook, Google)
- **Medium** - How it's shared (like email, post, ad)
- **Campaign** - The name of your campaign (like summer-sale)
- **Term** - Keywords (for paid ads)
- **Content** - To test different versions

**Quick Presets:**
- Ready-made settings for Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, Google Ads, Email, and TikTok

---

### 5. Bio Pages
Make a page with all your links in one place. Perfect for Instagram or TikTok bio.

**Blocks you can add:**
- **Header** - A title with your name or brand
- **Link** - A button that goes to a website
- **Social** - Icons for your social media
- **Text** - Write anything you want
- **Divider** - A line to separate sections
- **Image** - Add a picture
- **Contact** - Show your email or phone

**Themes:**
- Light (white background)
- Dark (black background)
- Gradient (colorful background)
- Minimal (simple and clean)
- Neon (bright colors)

---

### 6. Dashboard
See everything at a glance when you open the app.

**What you see:**
- Total clicks on all links
- Number of active links
- QR code scans
- Conversion rate
- Your recent links
- Quick buttons to create links

---

## Part 2: How to Use Each Feature

---

### How to Make a Short Link

**Step 1:** Click on "Links" in the menu

**Step 2:** Click the blue button that says "Create Link"

**Step 3:** Fill in the form:
- **Title** - Give your link a name (like "My YouTube Video")
- **Destination URL** - Paste your long link here
- **Custom Alias** - (Optional) Type a short name you want
- **Password** - (Optional) Add a password if you want
- **Expiry Date** - (Optional) Pick when the link should stop working

**Step 4:** Click "Create Link"

**Step 5:** Your new link is ready! Click the copy button to copy it.

---

### How to Make a QR Code

**Step 1:** Click on "QR Code" in the menu

**Step 2:** Pick what type of QR code you want:
- URL, Contact, WiFi, SMS, Email, or Text

**Step 3:** Fill in the information:
- For URL: paste your website link
- For Contact: type name and phone number
- For WiFi: type network name and password
- For SMS: type phone number and message
- For Email: type email address and subject
- For Text: type your message

**Step 4:** Choose colors (optional):
- Pick a color for the QR code
- Pick a background color

**Step 5:** Add a frame (optional):
- Choose a frame style
- Write text for the frame (like "Scan Me!")

**Step 6:** Click "Download PNG" or "Download SVG" to save it

---

### How to See Your Analytics

**Step 1:** Click on "Analytics" in the menu

**Step 2:** Pick a time range at the top:
- 1H = Last 1 hour
- 24H = Last 24 hours
- 7D = Last 7 days
- 30D = Last 30 days
- 90D = Last 90 days

**Step 3:** Look at the cards:
- **Total Clicks** - How many times your links were clicked
- **Unique Visitors** - How many different people clicked
- **Countries** - Where people are from
- **Top Referrer** - Where most people came from

**Step 4:** Scroll down to see charts:
- Clicks over time (line chart)
- Devices (pie chart)
- Browsers (pie chart)
- Countries list
- Referrers list
- Heat map (shows busy times)

---

### How to Use UTM Builder

**Step 1:** Click on "UTM Builder" in the menu

**Step 2:** Paste your website link in the first box

**Step 3:** Use a Quick Preset OR fill in manually:

**To use a preset:**
- Click "Facebook", "Twitter", or any other button
- The boxes will fill automatically

**To fill manually:**
- **Source:** Type where you'll share it (facebook, google, email)
- **Medium:** Type how you'll share it (social, cpc, email)
- **Campaign:** Type your campaign name (summer-sale, new-product)
- **Term:** (Optional) Type keywords
- **Content:** (Optional) Type to test different versions

**Step 4:** Copy the final URL from the green box at the bottom

**Step 5:** (Optional) Click "Save as Preset" to save your settings for later

---

### How to Make a Bio Page

**Step 1:** Click on "Bio Pages" in the menu

**Step 2:** Click "Create New Page"

**Step 3:** Give your page a name and pick a theme

**Step 4:** Add blocks by clicking the buttons:
- Click "Header" to add a title
- Click "Link" to add a button
- Click "Social" to add social media icons
- Click "Text" to add words
- Click "Divider" to add a line
- Click "Image" to add a picture
- Click "Contact" to add contact info

**Step 5:** Click on any block to edit it

**Step 6:** Drag blocks up or down to change the order

**Step 7:** Click "Preview" to see how it looks

**Step 8:** Copy the link and put it in your Instagram or TikTok bio

---

### How to Use the Dashboard

The dashboard shows you everything at once. Here's what each part means:

**Top Cards:**
- **Total Clicks** - All clicks on all your links
- **Active Links** - Links that are still working
- **QR Scans** - Times your QR codes were scanned
- **Conversion Rate** - Percentage of people who did what you wanted

**Recent Links:**
- Shows your newest links
- Click "View all" to see all your links

**Quick Actions:**
- **Create Short Link** - Make a new short link
- **Generate QR Code** - Make a new QR code
- **View Analytics** - See your stats

---

## Tips and Tricks

1. **Use good names** - Give your links clear names so you remember what they are

2. **Check your stats** - Look at analytics every week to see what's working

3. **Use UTM tags** - Add UTM tags to know which social media brings the most clicks

4. **Make it pretty** - Use colors on your QR codes that match your brand

5. **Keep it simple** - Don't add too many blocks to your bio page
