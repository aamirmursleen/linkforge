# Frequently Asked Questions

## General Questions

### What is LinkForge?
LinkForge is a link management platform that helps you shorten URLs, create QR codes, build bio pages, and track analytics for all your links.

### Is LinkForge free to use?
Yes! LinkForge offers a free plan with:
- 5 short links per month
- 2 QR codes per month
- Basic analytics
- 1 bio page

Upgrade to Pro for unlimited links, QR codes, and advanced features.

### How do I sign up?
1. Go to linkforge.io
2. Click "Start Free" or "Sign Up"
3. Enter your email and password
4. Verify your email
5. Start using LinkForge!

### Do I need a credit card for the free plan?
No, the free plan doesn't require a credit card.

## Link Questions

### How long do short links last?
Links on free plan last forever unless you delete them. They never expire unless you set an expiration date.

### Can I edit a link after creating it?
Yes! You can change:
- The destination URL
- Custom alias (if available)
- Password protection
- Expiration dates
- UTM parameters

### What happens when a link expires?
Expired links show an "expired" message. Users cannot access the destination. You can extend the expiration anytime.

### Can I password protect a link?
Yes! Add password protection when creating or editing a link. Users must enter the password to access the destination.

### What happens if someone enters the wrong password?
After 5 failed attempts, the link is locked for 15 minutes to prevent brute force attacks.

## QR Code Questions

### What size should I print my QR code?
Minimum recommended size is 2cm x 2cm (about 0.8 inches). For scanning from further away, print larger.

### Can I use my QR code for commercial purposes?
Yes, all QR codes you create are yours to use for any purpose.

### Do QR codes expire?
QR codes themselves don't expire. If the QR code links to a short link, it follows that link's expiration settings.

### Can I track QR code scans?
Yes! Create a short link first, then make a QR code for that link. All scans will be tracked in Analytics.

## Analytics Questions

### How accurate is the analytics data?
We track all clicks with high accuracy. We filter out known bots and crawlers to show only real human traffic.

### Is the data real-time?
Yes, analytics update within seconds of each click.

### How long is analytics data kept?
Analytics data is kept for 90 days on all plans.

### Can I export my analytics?
Yes, export to CSV or JSON format from the Analytics dashboard.

## Account Questions

### How do I change my password?
1. Go to Settings
2. Click "Security"
3. Click "Change Password"
4. Enter current and new password

### How do I enable two-factor authentication?
1. Go to Settings
2. Click "Security"
3. Click "Enable 2FA"
4. Scan QR code with authenticator app
5. Enter verification code

### Can I delete my account?
Yes, contact support to delete your account. All your links and data will be permanently removed.

### How do I change my email?
Contact support to change your account email address.

## Billing Questions

- Credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)

## Technical Questions

### What browsers are supported?
- Chrome (recommended)
- Firefox
- Safari
- Edge
- Opera

### Is there an API?
Yes! API access is available on Pro and Enterprise plans. See API documentation for details.

### Can I use a custom domain?
Yes, Pro and Enterprise plans support custom domains. Add your domain in Settings → Domains.

### How do I verify my custom domain?
1. Add a CNAME record pointing to our servers
2. We'll verify the DNS configuration
3. SSL certificate is provisioned automatically
